Open source · LoRaWAN · Long range, low power

Know what happens at home, before it becomes a problem.

LoRaLarm is an open-source IoT alarm and control system. Battery-powered sensors talk LoRaWAN over kilometers of range, and you decide what happens next: get notified, log it, or switch things remotely.

Monitor. Alert. Control.

Three verbs, one system. Every part is open, inspectable, and yours to modify.

monitor

Sense anything

Door contacts, motion, water leaks, temperature, or your own sensor firmware. Battery life measured in years, not days.

alert

Alert on your terms

Push, email, or MQTT into your existing home automation. Alerts are rules you own, not a subscription.

control

Act, remotely

Toggle relays, arm or disarm zones, and supervise it all over a link that reaches the far end of the property.

Status at a glance

Every dot and color means the same thing here as it does on your device. Documentation, dashboards, and hardware share one vocabulary.

OK / ARMED
Healthy, nominal, reporting on schedule.
WARNING
Weak signal, low battery, missed check-in.
ALERT
Triggered event. Needs your attention now.
OFFLINE
No contact within the expected interval.
